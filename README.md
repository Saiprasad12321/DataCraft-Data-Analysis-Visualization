# DataCraft — Consumer Behavior & Shopping Analytics

> Turning customer purchase data into actionable insights for better marketing, customer engagement, and retention decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**DataCraft** is an end-to-end consumer behavior analytics project focused on understanding how customers purchase, what influences their spending, and which factors are associated with stronger customer engagement and loyalty.

The project takes raw customer shopping data through a complete analytics workflow — from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to MySQL-based analysis and Power BI visualization.

The goal is to move beyond simply describing the data and translate analytical findings into practical business recommendations related to customer retention, promotions, product performance, subscriptions, and purchasing behavior.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of customer transaction data, but raw records do not directly explain:

- Who the most valuable customers are
- What products customers prefer
- How discounts relate to spending
- Whether shipping preferences differ across customer value
- How subscription customers behave
- How customers progress from first-time buyers to loyal customers

### Key Business Question

> **How can consumer shopping data be analyzed to identify purchasing patterns, understand customer value, and develop strategies that improve engagement and long-term loyalty?**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Clean and prepare raw customer shopping data for analysis.
2. Explore customer purchasing behavior and demographic patterns.
3. Identify relationships between discounts and customer spending.
4. Analyze product preferences and customer ratings.
5. Compare spending patterns across shipping methods.
6. Examine subscription behavior and customer loyalty.
7. Understand the distribution of new, returning, and loyal customers.
8. Convert analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
9. Present key insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 purchase records** and **18 attributes** covering customer demographics, purchasing behavior, products, transactions, and engagement-related information.

### Major Data Areas

- Customer demographics
- Age and gender
- Product categories
- Purchase amounts
- Purchase frequency
- Discounts
- Customer ratings
- Shipping preferences
- Subscription status
- Payment methods
- Customer loyalty behavior

### Data Quality

The dataset was examined for missing and inconsistent values during the data preparation stage.

The main missing-data issue identified was associated with the **Review Rating** field, which was addressed before continuing with the analysis.
